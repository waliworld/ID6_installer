iDrive 6 Gauge Installer

v0.2.9

Installs BoostDeck, ShiftDeck and MHD Maps onto your BMW NBT Evo (iDrive 6) head unit. Everything runs locally on this computer over the cable in your car — nothing leaves the vehicle.

Before you start — what you need
  • Car parked, ignition on, and iDrive fully awake. Keep it awake the whole time.
  • A wired ENET cable from the car's OBD port to this computer (a USB-to-Ethernet adapter is fine).
  • Temporary SSH enabled on the head unit using the Bavarian SSH Client for your exact firmware — you'll get a root password from it. Bavarian SSH Client
  • Supported firmware: NBTevo_N18092I, V20153I, or Y24073J. Other versions are blocked for safety.

This is a parked preview. After install, check the gauges and map switching on the car before relying on the readings.

Step 1

Find your car

Plug in the ENET cable, then press Find my car. If only the gateway fills in, enter the head-unit SSH IP from the Bavarian tool.

The gateway and head unit usually have different IPs on the 169.254.x.x ENET link.

Step 2

Unlock & verify compatibility

Enter the root password from the Bavarian SSH Client. The installer confirms the firmware and matches the on-car framework fingerprints before anything is written.

Where do I find the iDrive version?

Navigation → OPTIONS → Navigation settings → Position and version → Version information. The full code starts with NBTevo_. Menu wording can vary by car.

iDrive display resolution

Leave on Automatic unless detection fails. The resolution iDrive sends can differ from the panel's advertised size.

Step 3

Install the apps

Keep iDrive on and the cable connected. The installer uploads and reads back every file, keeping a backup of anything it replaces.
